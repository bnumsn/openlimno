## [2.1.0] — 2026-05-18

### Added
- **v2.1.0 — per-cell composite (true n-factor geometric mean)**:
    Closes the second of the two v1.10.1-deferred items by shipping the **true per-cell n-factor geometric mean** the 6-round review chain repeatedly flagged. v1.6.0—v1.10.1's `apply_overlay` works on the WUA-Q column with *basin-wide scalar* cover/thermal overlays — necessary because cover came from a per-region pixel-weighted mean and thermal came from a time-mean at a single station. v2.1.0 adds a parallel library-level surface that consumes **per-cell arrays** and computes the literature-standard composite at the per-cell level.
    - **`apply_overlay_per_cell(csi_dv_per_cell, area_per_cell, *, cover_si_per_cell=None, thermal_si_per_cell=None, method="geom_mean")`** in `src/openlimno/habitat/composite.py`. Returns a dict with `csi_total_per_cell` (the per-cell composite SI), `wua_base_m2` (Σ A_i · CSI_dv), `wua_composite_m2` (Σ A_i · CSI_total), `n_factors_per_cell`, and `composite_to_base_ratio`. Scalars broadcast across cells; arrays are checked for shape and ∈ [0, 1].
    - **`cover_si_per_section(lulc_tif, section_geometries)`** in `src/openlimno/habitat/cover.py`. Calls `cover_si_from_lulc_raster` once per supplied geometry and returns a 1-D numpy array of per-section SI values — the bridge from a LULC raster + a list of per-section riparian buffers to the per-cell composite input.
    - **Method contract explicitly differentiated**: `method="product"` always yields `composite ≤ base` (viability gate, carries v1.10.1 R6-2 forward). `method="geom_mean"` CAN yield `composite > base` at cells where CSI_dv is tiny but cover/thermal are strong — mathematically intrinsic to the per-cell geometric mean (the `^(1/n)` lifts near-zero values close to 1) and HABBY-standard. Geom-mean is a *softening* combination rule, not a viability gate; the composite is bounded above by Σ A_i (perfect-CSI ceiling). Both behaviours are pinned in `test_v210_apply_overlay_per_cell_product_never_inflates_wua` and `test_v210_apply_overlay_per_cell_geom_mean_bounded_by_wetted_area`.
    - **What v2.1.0 does NOT ship (staged for v2.2.0+)**: `Case.run` integration via a `composite_overlay_method = "geom_mean_per_cell"` schema key. The Case pipeline currently aggregates depth × velocity CSI inside `_compute_cell_wua` and returns only the reach total — wiring per-cell results through the rest of the pipeline (provenance, regulatory exports, watermark headers) is a separate refactor. **Per-cell thermal raster** also stays deferred — `thermal_metrics` currently emits a time-mean scalar from a single point time series, and true spatial T(x) is part of the 3.x research route.
    - 10 new tests in `test_wua_watermark.py` (`test_v210_*`): zero-overlay base case, product-method consistency with the v1.6.0 column-level basin-scalar product, uniform-overlay geom-mean cross-check, per-cell array vs broadcast-scalar distinguishability, product never inflates, geom-mean bounded by wetted area + can inflate (exercise the design property), out-of-range CSI rejection, shape-mismatch fail-loud, n_factors_per_cell metadata, and the empty cover_si_per_section contract.
    - 83 unit tests in `test_wua_watermark.py` (+10 vs v2.0.0). 0 lint findings in changed files.
    - **The "per-cell true geom_mean" deferral from v2.0.0 is now CLOSED at the library API level.** The Case-integration deferral (separate ship) remains; F11 cosmetic naming (the other 2.0 deferral) is unchanged.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-05-18

**OpenLimno's first stable major release.** v2.0.0 freezes the public API after a 6-round codex+gemini review chain that closed 28 of 30 substantive findings on the v1.6.0 — v1.10.1 composite-overlay + atomic-write surface area. No new features in this release — v2.0.0 is a cut at v1.10.1's HEAD with a version bump, a charter restatement, and the formal end of the "1.x rc" period.

### What 2.0 promises

* **Stable composite-overlay API** — `CompositeOverlay`, `apply_overlay(method=…)`, `composite_summary(method=…)`, and `habitat.composite_overlay_method ∈ {product, geom_mean}` in case.schema.json are now contract surface. Future breaking changes here require a 3.x bump.
* **Stable atomic-write contract** — every Case.run output writer (provenance.json, all CSV / parquet / netcdf / PNG artefacts, all `*_composite.csv` regulatory reports) goes through `Case._atomic_write(target, writer)` with umask-respecting permissions and chmod-before-replace ordering. Concurrent readers see content+permissions atomically together; crashes leave no partial files.
* **Stable HSI quality grading + watermarking** — A/B/C grades surface in `provenance.json`, get prepended to every regulatory CSV header, and apply red-banner watermarks to matplotlib outputs at C grade.
* **Stable regulatory exports** — CN-SL712, US-FERC-4e, EU-WFD all carry composite variants (`<kind>_composite.csv`) when overlays are present, with method-tagged headers so reviewers see the combination rule (`product` or `geom_mean`) directly in the artefact.

### What stays deferred to 3.x

* **F11**: `_composite_view` cosmetic column rename. Would force a coordinated update across regulatory_export modules with no functional benefit; re-evaluate if a public-API audit demands it.
* **True per-cell four-way geometric mean** (`(d × v × c × t)^(1/4)`): requires cover and thermal as per-cell rasters rather than basin-wide scalars. Out of scope for v1.x; flagged for the v3.x research-route work alongside the SPEC §4.2.2 per-cell version.
* **Strict mypy** + the upstream-stubs work on yaml / geopandas / rasterio. Pragmatic non-strict mode preserved through v2.0; strict mode is a v3.x project once the ecosystem catches up.

### Fixed
- **v1.10.1 — 6th-pass review patches (R6-1 + R6-2 + R6-3)**:
    6th-pass codex+gemini review of v1.10.0 returned PASS 1 N6 CLOSED (F11 deferred as instructed). Three new PASS 2 findings — two HIGH/MED on the geom_mean implementation, one LOW on the CHANGELOG. This point release closes all three.
    - **R6-1 (HIGH gemini / MED codex): geom_mean produced NaN for all-zero WUA columns.** Both reviewers independently flagged that v1.10.0's `area_proxy = float(np.nanmean(base[base>0])) or 1.0` fallback was broken: when every base WUA value was zero, `np.nanmean` returned `nan`, and `float(nan) or 1.0` evaluated to `nan` because nan is truthy in Python. The whole composite column became NaN and `composite_summary.idxmax` then raised "Encountered all NA values". Fixed by withdrawing the `area_proxy` reach-scale linearisation entirely.
    - **R6-2 (HIGH gemini): geom_mean inflated composite above base.** gemini's verification: using a sweep-mean `area_proxy` as a scalar reweighting factor caused `base^(1/n) · overlay_geom · area_proxy^((n-1)/n)` to mathematically exceed the hydraulic base WUA at low-flow discharges where `base_Q < area_proxy`. A suitability overlay must never inflate WUA — only dampen it. The v1.10.0 formula was a reach-scale linearisation of a per-cell rule that only holds when CSI is approximately uniform across the wetted area; for non-uniform sweeps it failed both directions.
    - **Fix: geom_mean redesigned as column-level n-th-root softening.** New formula: `WUA_composite(Q) = WUA_dv(Q) · (SI_T · SI_C)^(1/n)` where `n = 1 + n_overlays`. The overlay enters as the n-th root rather than the raw product (which is the `product` method), recovering HABBY's "softer than product" property at the column level — the only level where SI_T / SI_C are defined when they are basin-wide scalars. Monotone in base, bounded by `base · 1 = base` (no inflation, ever), and free of NaN propagation paths. The **true** per-cell four-way geometric mean `(d × v × c × t)^(1/4)` still requires cover/thermal as per-cell rasters and remains flagged for v2.x.
    - **R6-3 (LOW codex): CHANGELOG test-count text corrected.** v1.10.0 claimed "10 new tests" / "79 unit tests in test_wua_watermark.py"; actual collected count was 11 new (`test_v1100_*`) and 69 total. Fixed in this v1.10.1 entry.
    - 4 new tests in `test_wua_watermark.py`: `test_v1101_geom_mean_all_zero_base_does_not_propagate_nan` (pins R6-1), `test_v1101_geom_mean_never_inflates_above_base` across a heavily-skewed WUA-Q sweep (pins R6-2), `test_v1101_geom_mean_softens_overlay_by_nth_root` (pins the new column-level formula), and `test_v1101_geom_mean_single_overlay_softens_less_than_double` (pins monotonicity in n).
    - Real-data smoke: `examples/composite_hsi/quickstart.py` rerun. Old geom_mean headline values were `74.96` (juv) / `138.33` (spawn) — those were the inflation/dampening-mix artefacts of the withdrawn reach-scale linearisation. New geom_mean headlines: `202.68` (juv) / `262.94` (spawn) = `base_max × 0.6411` where `0.6411 = (0.62·0.4255)^(1/3)`. All composite values now sit cleanly between the v1.6.0 product line (`83.37` / `108.15`) and the base hydraulic line (`316.02` / `409.97`).
    - 73 unit tests in test_wua_watermark.py (+4 vs v1.10.0). 0 ruff findings in changed files.
